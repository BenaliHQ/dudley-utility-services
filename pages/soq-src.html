Our Technology · Kudu

Project intelligence and visibility for risk management.

Kudu is the land records, project management, and GIS platform behind every DUS project, connecting parcel data, milestones, documentation, and reporting in one system. Status moves when work happens, so you see every risk on the right-of-way while there's still time to manage it, and reporting reaches you the way you want it.

  • Custom Project Reporting
  • Land Records & Document Management
  • GIS Mapping & Parcel Data
