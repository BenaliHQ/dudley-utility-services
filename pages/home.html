What we do

Strategic land solutions across the utility project lifecycle

We secure the land rights your project is built on, then stay through construction and closeout.

Routing & siting

Route selection and due diligence, with the estimate built parcel by parcel on per-acre valuation.

Public relations & community outreach

We knock doors, meet landowner associations, and attend open houses and council meetings.

Title research

Surface and mineral ownership cured before offers go out, so documents name the current owner.

Survey support & easement acquisition

Survey permission and rights of entry, then easements and fee acquisitions closed on good-faith offers.

Construction

Notice before crews cross, gates and damages managed, and a point of contact who always answers.

Operations

Landowner support long after energization: land records, encroachments, and vegetation managed.
