Public Relations & Community Outreach

Title Research

Our Technology · Kudu

Project intelligence and visibility for risk management.

Kudu is the land records, project management, and GIS platform behind every DUS project, connecting parcel data, milestones, documentation, and reporting in one system. Status moves when work happens, so if a challenge surfaces on the right-of-way, there's still time to manage it, and reporting reaches you the way you want it.

  • Custom Project Reporting
  • Land Records & Document Management
  • GIS Mapping & Parcel Data
  • Budget & Payment Tracking
