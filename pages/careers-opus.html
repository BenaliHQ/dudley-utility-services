Life at DUS

What working here actually feels like

We're privately owned. That means we can decide what's right for the people who work here and then go do it, without asking anyone outside this company first. Here's what that looks like day to day.

Privately owned

Nobody outside this company sets our headcount, our pay, or how long we spend training someone. We decide, and we live with it. That's why a good idea from a first-year agent can change how the whole team works.

Culture at every level

Dudley Land Company built this over 45 years: empowered leadership, trust, and integrity. Garrett Gill, our EVP, says it plainly: "We don't just talk culture, we live it." You'll find it embodied at every level, no matter the position.

We build the whole career

New land professionals get a mentor, weekly check-ins, and a six-month program built around roughly 100 checkpoints you need exposure to before you stand on your own. We build people from internship through the end of a career.
