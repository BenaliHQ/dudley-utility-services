The wordmark is artwork.

A custom condensed logotype, delivered as files. It is placed, never re-typed, never restyled. Black and green on light grounds; white and green on dark.

Motifs

Four moves make it DUS.

The survey grid, the route line, the horizon rule, and the corner ticks. Each is drawn from the work itself: plat maps, corridors, section lines.

Survey grid · dark

Every dark field carries the plat grid: fine 44px cells, a heavier section line every fourth. The green horizon anchors the bottom edge.

Survey grid · light

On white it is a whisper, not a pattern. If you notice it before the content, it is too strong.

Route line

Drawn like an alignment sheet: dash-dot centerline, easement corridor, PI monuments, station ticks, parcel lines. One per composition, behind content, never through body text.

Service Overview
Corner ticks

Plat-sheet corners frame the body of print documents. Quiet, structural, surveyor-precise.
